# X3Paranoids

X3Paranoids is a first-person vector tank game for the RayNeo X3 Pro AR glasses, an homage to the 1982 arcade cabinet Tron tie-in *Space Paranoids*: you pilot a tank through a phosphor-green wireframe maze, hunting patrolling Recognizers before they hunt you. The wearer's head serves as the periscope — looking around actually turns the view, world-locked to the maze — while the temple pad drives and fires. Recognizers turn red and must physically swing their cab to face you before firing, and getting caught triggers a full pull-back "stomp" capture sequence rather than simple destruction. A recurring energy well relocates itself every time it's drained, turning "refill now or chase the Bit" into a fresh risk calculation each wave, and every in-game announcement is spoken by one of two distinct synthesized voices — a flat, machine-processed system voice and a separate TTS-rendered "pilot" voice — scheduled so they never talk over each other.

## Controls (right temple pad)

| Gesture | In play | In the settings menu |
|---|---|---|
| Tap | fire (or fight free of a capture) | adjust / toggle the selected row |
| Swipe up / down, flick | dash forward / backward | move the selection |
| Swipe up / down, hold | cruise forward / backward until released | — |
| Swipe left / right | quarter-turn the hull 90° | adjust the selected value |
| Double-tap | — (settings are off the arena) | close |
| Triple-tap | re-centre the head | — |
